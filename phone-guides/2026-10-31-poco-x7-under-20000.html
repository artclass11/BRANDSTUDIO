Why this is our one pick

01

A genuinely good screen and speakers

The 6.67-inch curved AMOLED panel has 1.5K resolution and a 120Hz refresh rate. Independent testing by 91mobiles praised its brightness, colour and stereo speakers, making it especially appealing for films, video and everyday browsing.

02

Capable everyday performance and useful 5G support

The Dimensity 7300 Ultra with 8GB RAM is comfortable for messaging, streaming, browsing and normal multitasking. The India listing specifies 5G SA/NSA support across bands including n1, n3, n5, n8, n28, n40, n41, n77 and n78.

03

OIS camera, practical battery and solid protection

The 50MP main camera includes optical image stabilisation, alongside an 8MP ultra-wide camera. The India variant is listed with a 5,500mAh battery and 45W charging; the phone also has Gorilla Glass Victus 2 and an IP68 rating. Expect the best camera results in good light.

One important limitation

Not ideal for heavy gaming or buyers who want the longest software runway.

91mobiles observed frame drops in demanding games and softer, noisier low-light photos. It also noted that the advertised three-year OS-update window amounts to only two major Android-version upgrades from the Android 14 launch version. HyperOS includes pre-installed apps; some can be removed.

Who should buy it?

Choose the POCO X7 5G if your everyday priorities are a sharp, smooth display, stereo sound, 5G, daylight photography and a capable all-round experience. Skip it if high-frame-rate gaming, consistently strong night photography or a cleaner software experience is your top priority.
